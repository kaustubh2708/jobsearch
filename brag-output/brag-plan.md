# Brag Plan: Orbit

## What is this app?
Orbit is a local-first job search site where you pick the companies you'd love to join (or stay open to any above your pay floor), a hatted detective agent hunts career portals, LinkedIn posts and job platforms, an analyst and a verifier filter and double-check, and your tracker shows Jobs available with referral options beside them.

## The angle
A tiny agent factory working a job search for you, told as one clear story with real product screens: **choose → detective hunts → filter by fit → verify every link → Jobs available with reach-out options → you stay in control.** The charm is the small robot crew (the detective in a hat with a magnifier). The honesty is the punchline: Orbit drafts, but never applies or messages for you.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
"Pick your companies." types in large editorial type, then "Let a detective hunt." as the hatted detective robot pops up beside it holding a magnifier.

## Key moments (the middle)
- Company blocks, 5 per row, each with HR/TA, Alumni and Eng contact badges. Blocks get selected one by one. A pay-floor chip ("Min pay · 35 LPA") and an "Open to any company" toggle sit above.
- The detective scans three boards: Career portals, LinkedIn posts, Job platforms. Little job cards fly into him.
- The belt: the Analyst scores fit ("Fit 88 · skills match"); a low-fit card drops into "Not a fit". The Verifier checks links ("✓ Verified live"); one card shows "✗ Link broken" and drops into its bin.
- Tracker, Jobs available: a company dropdown opens a job (fit, pay, "✓ Verified live", Open listing, stage, Notes), with the Reach out panel on the side (HR/TA, Alumni, Engineers, a draft note with Copy and "I sent it").

## Outro / punchline
"You review. You send." Then: "Orbit never applies or messages for you." Final lockup: orbit. + "Pick your companies. Let a detective hunt."

## User flow worth showing
Entry: choose company blocks (or open to any + min pay) → Key action: the hunt runs (detective → analyst → verifier) → Result: Jobs available on the tracker, with a reach-out draft ready to copy.

## Tone
- Preset: default
- Creative direction: a warm, quietly confident factory-floor story with small robot characters, never hype.
- Interpretation: clean pacing, 6 scenes, readable holds, no fake numbers, restrained SFX, product UI as the star.

## Audio direction
- Role: warm bed with sparse professional accents
- Music: happy-beats-business-moves-vol-9 (≈115 BPM)
- Music treatment: starts at 0, low-to-medium bed (~0.5), fades out over the last 1.5s
- Music cue guidance: bundled preset read. Strong cues at 3.70s (scene 2), 7.92s (scene 3), 12.65s (analyst/verifier payoff). Beat grid ≈0.53s spacing used for block selection ticks and card reveals.
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle; bass lightly warms the scene backdrop glow and breathes the robots, treble adds a soft shadow to the active card. No waveform/equalizer visuals.
- SFX posture: sparse, motion-matched
- Audio-coupled moments: block selection clicks, card drops into bins, tracker row reveals, final lockup
- Restraint rule: no sound on every beat; keep the voice-less story calm
