## Intent

Create a silent, caption-led, 30-second animated explainer for the Orbit homepage. Tell the complete user journey: capture a resume and preferences (skills, location, experience, compensation); scan a broad pool of opportunities across configured sources; score relevance and verify links; place the shortlist in the candidate's role tracker; show application status and referral/HR/TA context; then end with future application/outreach assistance explicitly marked work in progress. Use small friendly animated characters and clear, readable interface-inspired cards. The visual tone follows the site's warm ivory, evergreen, stone, and muted terracotta palette.

## Customizations

- Silent, text-led motion graphics; no voiceover or music. The video must make sense with sound off.
- Show broad search scale as an illustrative pool, not a factual live counter. Do not claim a verified daily number of roles scanned.
- Referral context is drawn from user-supplied tracker contacts, not scraped LinkedIn people or connection databases.
- Show a 4-day follow-up as a planned reminder/check-in cue, not as a currently delivered notification feature.
- Show future application and outreach assistance as **WORK IN PROGRESS**, with an explicit human-approval gate; do not depict messages or applications being sent automatically.
- Build a first cut directly; review the rendered preview before final rendering.

## Notes

- Avoid dark/neon styling, dense copy, tiny labels, and false claims of live automation.
- The source workbook/dashboard may not prove the actual number or cadence of daily searches; use non-quantified broad-scan language.
- Keep privacy and user control clear throughout.
