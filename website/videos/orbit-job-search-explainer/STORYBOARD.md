## Video direction

- Silent, caption-led; the on-screen phrases carry the complete narrative.
- Warm editorial palette follows `frame.md`: evergreen for trusted action, warm ivory for canvas, stone for secondary surfaces, terracotta as a scarce roadmap accent.
- One friendly, small geometric guide-bot appears as a distinct character in each beat; character gestures and purposeful UI movement, never random floating or perpetual loops.
- Smooth, deliberate entrances; reveal one meaningful element at a time to the on-screen copy. Favor still holds after each reveal. No neon, purple AI glow, fake job-count metrics, browser chrome, or tiny dense copy. The final referral-contact beat is the deliberate quiet hold before the closing roadmap.
- Never imply LinkedIn profile/connection scraping, hidden relationship lookups, or automatic outreach. Contacts shown come only from the user-supplied/verified contact list.
- The Day 4 follow-up prompt is marked PLANNED. Application/outreach support is marked WORK IN PROGRESS and always shows a human approval step.
- All key text stays above the bottom caption-safe band. The timeline is illustrative, not a live system status or verified daily throughput count.
