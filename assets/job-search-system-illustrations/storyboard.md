# Job-search factory — illustration sequence

Eight standalone 16:9 stills for a later ~36-second explainer. The story is designed for a service with many users: each person has separate preferences, matching rules, and private tracking. The first seven images explain the intended end-to-end workflow; image 08 is explicitly marked as planned.

| # | Image | Story beat | Suggested hold |
|---|---|---|---:|
| 1 | `01-candidate-setup.png` | Each user's resume and preferences enter their own profile drawer. | 4s |
| 2 | `02-multi-source-discovery.png` | The factory gathers job listings from employer sites, job boards, and visible job posts. | 4s |
| 3 | `03-normalize-deduplicate.png` | Repeated copies of one requisition are compared and reduced to one role record. | 4s |
| 4 | `04-per-user-fit-gates.png` | The same role is evaluated against separate users' private criteria; uncertain cases go to review. | 5s |
| 5 | `05-verify-evidence.png` | A listing is checked against page-level evidence and routed to active, manual-review, or closed. | 5s |
| 6 | `06-fit-and-pay-evidence.png` | Role fit is assessed separately from published pay, estimates, and unknown salary. | 4s |
| 7 | `07-tracker-and-human-action.png` | Users track applications, follow-ups, and referral options; the person makes the external action. | 5s |
| 8 | `08-planned-human-approval.png` | Planned freshness checks scale the service while a human approval checkpoint remains. | 5s |

Suggested later motion: slow lateral camera moves between factory stations, cards moving on belts, a small magnifier pass at verification, and a final pause on the human approval lever. Keep each image as its own shot; these are not a single contact sheet. The drawings are conceptual illustrations, not a claim that every depicted agent stage is currently automated.
