# Job Search Factory — visual direction

- Canvas: 1920 × 1080, 16:9.
- Existing artwork: warm-white paper, hand-drawn black linework, restrained red / blue / green accents. Preserve the artwork as the dominant visual.
- Supporting palette: warm paper `#F7F4EC`, ink `#202624`, muted sage `#416B58`, white `#FFFDF8`.
- Typeface: `system-ui, -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", sans-serif`; bold, large labels and short body copy.
- Captions: one dark, warm-ink panel anchored in the lower-left safe area; rounded corners, generous padding, small numbered stage label, large headline, and one concise explanatory line.
- Motion: slow, subtle Ken Burns drift on the illustration; caption panel slides in and settles. Hard cuts between stages. No neon, Chinese glyphs, fake UI, or decorative motion unrelated to the process.
- Sound: deliberate silence for this first pass; no audio assets were supplied and local voice/music engines are unavailable.
