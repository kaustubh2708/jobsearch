01 / Candidate setup

Start with the person.

Resume, location, role, salary, and preferences shape a private profile.

02 / Opportunity discovery

Search across sources.

Company career pages, job boards, and permitted public job posts.

03 / Clean records

Make listings comparable.

Normalize role details, reconcile IDs, and remove duplicates.

04 / Candidate-specific fit

Match each profile independently.

Skills, experience, location, and constraints decide the fit.

05 / Evidence verification

Verify before calling it open.

Confirm the exact role; send blocked or closed links to the right status.

06 / Fit and pay evidence

Keep facts separate from estimates.

Show role alignment and label salary as published, estimated, or unknown.

07 / Human-led tracking

Track progress. You take action.

Applications, follow-ups, and referral paths stay under your control.

08 / Planned — human checkpoint

Scale carefully, keep approval human.

Freshness checks are planned; no automatic applications or messages.
